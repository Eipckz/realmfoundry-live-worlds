# RealmFoundry v0.3.0 — The Tycoon Rebuild

This release replaces the earlier third-person presentation with the intended top-down MMORPG management game.

## Highlights

- Elevated strategy camera with WASD panning and mouse-driven construction.
- Six selectable, placeable service categories: Inn, Smithy, Uplink, Guild Hall, Dungeon Gate, and Travel Dock.
- Reactive cash, subscriber, hype, and changelog progression.
- A complete six-building objective followed by a player-triggered major update.
- New original fantasy town authored in Blender: 523 scene objects, 22 materials, layered terrain, waterways, roads, bridges, market details, and service landmarks.
- Corrected HUD and business-facing language throughout the playable loop.
- Full Windows Development package with visible build/release confirmation banners.

## Controls

- `W` `A` `S` `D`: pan camera
- `1`–`6`: select a service
- Left mouse button: place selected service
- `R`: publish the major update after all six categories are built
